    Viewing history

    Race to Finish records what each person watches, for races and their own stats. Each person's history is private to them. You only see someone's history if they turn on sharing in their My Activity tab.

    …

    Brings in what Jellyfin already knows: each person's watched items, the activity log (kept 30 days by default, see Dashboard → Logs), and the Playback Reporting plugin if installed. Safe to run again. Imported history never counts toward races.

    Ready-made Top 5 challenges

    The Top 5 movies of 2020 to 2025 come from TMDB, using the TMDB key Jellyfin already has. Nothing to set up. Open a year to see its films. A year can be turned on once all 5 are on your server; turning it on locks its list. The races themselves arrive in the next update: years you turn on now will open as races then.
